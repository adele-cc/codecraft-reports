Summary

Beldex engaged CodeCraft in July 2026 with three objectives: to raise the smart social metrics that exchanges and investors use to judge a project's market presence, to grow and activate its X, Discord and Telegram communities, and to prepare the ground for tier-1 derivatives listings, using its $8M funding round as a catalyst.

CodeCraft ran nine workstreams in parallel from the campaign launch on 28 July. A Beldex-branded quest platform and 200 partner-community collaborations brought new users in and gave them a reason to stay. Two creator programmes on CodeCraft's Nucleus platform, an open global leaderboard and a curated private KOL leaderboard, put a sustained volume of content about Beldex into the X feed. In parallel, the PR team carried four announcements into global, Korean and Chinese media and prepared Beldex's executives as spokespeople.

Ahead of Korea Blockchain Week, a three-week Korean-language campaign and on-site support established Beldex in the Korean market. Throughout the engagement, CodeCraft's advisory team met Beldex weekly on exchange strategy, liquidity and market structure, opened relationships with exchanges, market makers and investors, and onboarded a market-making partner. A CoinMarketCap content programme was added in September.

The result is a materially stronger market presence. Average views per post on @BeldexCoin increased tenfold and Cookie3 smart reach 24-fold. The number of smart accounts engaging with Beldex rose from 285 to 2,442, including 265 investors and 375 builders. Beldex ranked #1 in smart mentions on Nucleus in August, and its X and Discord communities grew by 86% and 81% respectively.

What CodeCraft built

CodeCraft set up a white-label quest platform on Beldex's own domain (quest.beldex.io) and configured it end to end: wallet-connected social quests, a public points leaderboard, point multipliers for the launch week and recurring check-ins. The platform went live alongside the Beldex trailer on 28 July, with the single call to action to join the loyalty programme.

Quests were rotated weekly to match the campaign calendar, covering X activity, community joins, partner collaborations, a LinkedIn quest and a cross-collaboration with Perceptron's quest platform. When X removed farming accounts in September, CodeCraft added a filter so flagged accounts could no longer earn points. At Beldex's request the platform remains open to support adoption of the new Extension Wallet.

Two complementary creator programmes ran on CodeCraft's Nucleus platform: an open global leaderboard to build volume and mindshare, and a curated private leaderboard to place Beldex in front of the high-signal accounts that exchanges and investors follow.

Nucleus global leaderboard · 29 Jul – 29 Sep

The global leaderboard opened Beldex to any creator on X. Nucleus scored every post, quote and reply on mindshare, reach, engagement quality and the quality of the accounts engaging, and gated participation on on- and off-chain metrics to filter low-quality accounts. Approach

Beldex had little presence in the Korean channels that drive retail attention. CodeCraft shortlisted Korean KOLs across Telegram, X and Naver blogs, with a combined following of around 285K, and prepared a briefing kit so every post carried an accurate, consistent message. Naver blogs were included because they rank in Korean search and keep Beldex discoverable after the campaign window.

The campaign was structured as a three-week narrative, moving the Korean audience from awareness to product understanding to technical confidence, and was tracked post by post in a daily-updated tracker shared with Beldex.

Results

38 posts were delivered, 2 more than agreed at no extra cost, across 14 Korean KOL channels (13 of which posted in more than one week) and 10 Naver blog posts. The 28 view-tracked Telegram and X posts recorded 40,031 views, with the strongest posts on CEN (3,500), WeCrypto (3,000 and 2,900) and Clever (2,094).

The three weeks recorded 14,321, 11,824 and 13,886 views respectively: a steady presence across 21 days rather than a single spike.

How the advisory worked

The engagement began with an 8-week go-to-market timeline that aligned community building, KOL campaigns, PR, social media and exchange outreach week by week. Exchange feedback gathered in July identified the readiness points to address before applying for derivatives listings, including DEX liquidity depth and valuation relative to venue expectations, alongside the social and mindshare signals the campaigns were built to move.

From there, CodeCraft built a sequenced listing plan, prepared a consolidated evidence pack for exchange conversations, selected and onboarded a non-directional market maker, and opened relationships with exchanges, liquidity providers and investors in parallel.

Status

The roadmap, exchange report and relationships are in place, and the market maker is connected. Perps applications, starting with exchanges such as OKX, Binance and more, are due to begin once Beldex successfully hits FDV targets as part of the agreed MM / FDV strategy put forth by CodeCraft Group.

CoinMarketCap Programme

CoinMarketCap is where many buyers research a token before they act. The programme puts Beldex content in front of that audience, written for CMC rather than reposted from X.

Strategy

CodeCraft ran the CMC onboarding call and delivered a CMC content strategy on 25 September. The approach is two to three long-form, CMC-native posts a week across three pillars, supported by posts from CMC KOLs. Posts tag relevant partner tokens so they also appear on those tokens' CMC feeds, extending reach beyond Beldex's own followers.

Alongside the content plan, CodeCraft walked Beldex through the advertising inventory on CoinMarketCap, CoinGecko, Dextools and GMGN, and investigated Beldex's CMC ranking with CMC's team.

Delivery

The first content batch was delivered on 30 September as a Notion content calendar. Each post was supplied as finished copy with a short rationale and mapped against Beldex's X calendar so CMC and X announcements land together. The calendar is now being aligned with Beldex's October releases: the Extension Wallet, the Web3.js SDK, Privacy Tokens and the Grants Program.
